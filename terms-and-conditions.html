2. About Y-Learn

Y-Learn is a mobile learning platform designed to support students and lecturers in managing courses, learning sessions, quizzes, link-based assignments, grades, academic feedback, and learning analytics.

3. User Roles

Y-Learn supports different user roles, including:

  • Students: users who access courses, participate in learning sessions, complete quizzes, submit assignment links, view grades, and receive feedback.
  • Lecturers: users who manage courses, sessions, quizzes, assignments, enrollments, submissions, grading, feedback, and learning analytics.

5. Acceptable Use

Users agree to use Y-Learn only for lawful, ethical, and academic purposes. Users must not:

  • Misuse the application to disrupt learning activities or compromise system security.

6. Assignment Submissions

Y-Learn supports link-based assignment submission. Students are responsible for ensuring that submitted links are correct, accessible, relevant to the assignment, and available for lecturer review.

Lecturers may review submissions, request revisions, provide feedback, save grades, finalize grades, or withdraw final grades according to academic workflow and course policy.

7. Quizzes and Attempts

Quizzes may include attempt limits, passing scores, time limits, and scoring rules. Students are responsible for completing quizzes honestly and according to course instructions.

Quiz attempts and scores may be stored as academic records and may be used for grade calculation, learning progress, analytics, and lecturer review.

8. Grades and Academic Records

Grades, final grades, revision records, quiz attempts, assignment submissions, and lecturer feedback may be stored as official or supporting academic records.

Once a final grade is assigned by a lecturer, the related submission may be locked from further student edits unless the lecturer withdraws or changes the final grade according to the available academic workflow.

9. Lecturer Responsibilities

Lecturers are responsible for:

  • Managing course content and learning activities appropriately.
  • Providing fair and accurate grading and feedback.
  • Using student data only for academic and course-related purposes.
  • Maintaining academic integrity in course management and assessment.

10. Student Responsibilities

Students are responsible for:

  • Completing quizzes and assignments honestly.
  • Submitting valid and accessible assignment links.
  • Following academic instructions, deadlines, and revision requirements.
