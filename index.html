Y-Learn Legal Documents

This repository hosts the public legal documents for the Y-Learn mobile application. Y-Learn is a mobile learning platform designed to support students and lecturers in managing courses, learning sessions, quizzes, link-based assignments, grades, academic feedback, and learning analytics.

Public Legal Pages

Privacy Policy

Explains how Y-Learn collects, uses, stores, and protects user data, including academic records and personal information of students and lecturers.

https://yeriksinggalen.github.io/ylearn-legal/privacy-policy.html
Terms and Conditions

Covers the terms of use for Y-Learn, including account responsibilities, acceptable use, and platform usage terms for students and lecturers.

https://yeriksinggalen.github.io/ylearn-legal/terms-and-conditions.html
Account Deletion New

Provides instructions and information on how users can request deletion of their Y-Learn account and associated data.

https://yeriksinggalen.github.io/ylearn-legal/account-deletion.html
